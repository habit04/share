Required corrections, with acceptance cases:

1. Account for all memory before allocation, not just queued input.
   host.cjs defaults to two active engines, counts queued input only, and
   qpdf-child.cjs checks output growth after callMain has already populated
   MEMFS. The linear-memory maximum does not cap JS/MEMFS/IPC allocations.
   Use a single active transform unless a revised aggregate budget is admitted.
   Define and enforce stage reservations for active/queued inputs, transfers,
   MEMFS input/output and readback. Stop output growth before the allocation;
   a stat check before readback alone is insufficient. Queue and memory
   refunds must be proven for success, refusal, cancellation and disposal.
   Keep the admitted WASM cap and work/deadline limits unchanged.

2. Disposal must cancel and join active jobs.
   host.cjs:219 currently drains only queued jobs. An independent fake-child
   case proves dispose returns with a live child, never kills it, and accepts
   its later successful output. Track active jobs, abort/kill them, reject
   late output, and join their exit before disposal finishes or slots reuse.
   Exercise disposal with both active and queued jobs; ordinary successful
   exit and explicit abort remain positive controls.

3. Preserve actual child exit outcomes and bound termination failure.
   host.cjs onExit ignores its argument, while settleWith kills even a child
   that just reported success. A valid-looking output followed by exit 17
   currently resolves successfully. Require the documented successful exit
   outcome; do not erase it by killing the successful path preemptively.
   Timeout/abort must still kill and join. A failed kill or absent exit must
   enter an explicit unavailable/quarantined state, not hang callers forever
   or reuse an unconfirmed slot. Add output-then-error, nonzero exit, crash,
   send/error-channel, no-exit and late-output cases.

4. Retire registrations with their save ticket and owner.
   registry.cjs has no cancel/owner-retirement API. Cancellation before the
   queued producer reaches consume leaves its registration until TTL/prune;
   repeated cancelled exports can exhaust all 64 records. Retire on admission
   cancellation/discard, renderer destruction, window shutdown and host
   disposal. Keep the hash/length/owner/destination binding fail-closed.
   Prove more than 64 register-then-cancel attempts do not exhaust the service,
   and that retired registrations can never authorize a later write.

5. Gate unsupported Plot choices explicitly.
   src/ui/plot.ts exposes the checkbox for every PDF dialog. Selecting it and
   then Raster image reaches an ordinary raster write with no explanation;
   the restricted vector branch is bypassed. Show/enable the option only
   for the supported Windows-desktop vector capability, or explicitly clear
   or refuse an incompatible choice with guidance before choosing/writing a
   destination. Test vector/raster switching, unsupported/browser bridges,
   no qualifying face, cancel/retry and the real Plot/IPC/save route.

6. Repair the independent verifier and its evidence claims.
   verify.py:212 uses `ed.get('/EncryptMetadata') is not False`. A pypdf
   BooleanObject(False) passes that expression. Resolve and validate the
   actual PDF boolean value, including absent/default and malformed types.
   The verifier also misses font widths/mappings: changing the plaintext
   /W [1 [650 650]] to /W [1 [750 650]], preserving all byte lengths/xref
   offsets, still passes 33/33 against the unchanged encrypted fixture.
   Compare font resources by page/resource role, including Encoding,
   CIDToGIDMap, widths/default widths, descriptor relationships and the
   embedded program/ToUnicode; do not collapse distinct resources merely by
   BaseFont. Add corrupt-width/mapping, false-metadata and broken-reference
   negative controls that fail. Correct the unsupported viewer-tested line
   in PDF-FONT-EMBEDDING.md, or provide the corresponding actual receipts.
